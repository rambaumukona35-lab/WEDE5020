Famous Races in History

Relive the most thrilling, dramatic, and unforgettable moments in motorsport history.


1998 Belgian Grand Prix
Date: August 30, 1998
Circuit: Circuit de Spa-Francorchamps, Belgium
Winner: Damon Hill (Jordan)
Iconic Moment: Michael Schumacher crashed into the back of David Coulthard in heavy rain. Schumacher stormed to the McLaren garage furious.
Fun Fact: Only 8 cars finished the race due to the massive first-lap pileup.


2008 Brazilian Grand Prix
Date: November 2, 2008
Circuit: Interlagos, São Paulo, Brazil
Winner: Felipe Massa (Ferrari)
Iconic Moment: Lewis Hamilton passed Timo Glock on the final corner of the final lap to win his first World Championship by 1 point.
Fun Fact: Felipe Massa crossed the line as champion — for 30 seconds, until Hamilton crossed the line.


1976 Japanese Grand Prix
Date: October 24, 1976
Circuit: Fuji Speedway, Japan
Winner: Mario Andretti (Lotus)
Iconic Moment: Niki Lauda refused to race in torrential rain after his horrific crash earlier that year. James Hunt needed 3rd place to win the title — and got it.
Fun Fact: This battle became the movie "Rush" (2013).


2021 Abu Dhabi Grand Prix
Date: December 12, 2021
Circuit: Yas Marina Circuit, Abu Dhabi
Winner: Max Verstappen (Red Bull)
Iconic Moment: Race director allowed only lapped cars between Hamilton and Verstappen to unlap. Verstappen on fresh tires passed Hamilton on final lap to win his first title.
Fun Fact: Most controversial finish in F1 history. Mercedes protested the result.


1984 Monaco Grand Prix
Date: June 3, 1984
Circuit: Circuit de Monaco
Winner: Alain Prost (McLaren)
Iconic Moment: Ayrton Senna chased down Prost in torrential rain. The race was stopped early due to conditions — Senna was furious because he was about to pass.
Fun Fact: Senna later said this was the race that motivated him to become the best in the wet.
