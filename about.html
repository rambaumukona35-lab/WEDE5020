About Racing Legends

Your ultimate destination for motorsport history, legends, and exclusive merchandise.


Our Story

Racing Legends was founded in 2015 by a group of passionate motorsport fans who wanted to preserve and celebrate the greatest moments in racing history.

What started as a small blog sharing classic race highlights has grown into the world's largest online destination for racing enthusiasts. Today, we reach over 50,000 fans monthly across 30+ countries.

Our mission is simple: keep the spirit of racing alive for new generations. We honor the fearless icons who pushed speed, skill, and courage to the limit — from the golden era to modern masters.
